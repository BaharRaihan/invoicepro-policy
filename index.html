InvoicePro Privacy Policy

InvoicePro ("the app", "we") is an offline-first invoicing app for small businesses and freelancers, published by the developer of InvoicePro. This page explains what data the app handles and why.

In short: InvoicePro stores your invoices, clients, expenses and business profile only on your own device. We never see this data, and there is no account or sign-in. The only outside data flow is the advertising shown in the free version.

Data you create in the app

Everything you enter — invoices, clients, expense records, your business profile, logo, signature, and settings — is stored locally on your device using a private on-device database. This data is never uploaded to us or to any server we operate. It leaves your device only when you choose to:

  • Share or save a generated PDF (using Android's own share sheet, WhatsApp, or email — apps you pick yourself);
  • Export a backup file to a location you choose, or import one you previously created;
  • Export a CSV report to a location you choose.

We do not require or collect your name, phone number, email address, or any account credentials to use InvoicePro.

Permissions the app uses

PermissionWhy
Internet / network stateNeeded to load and measure the ads described below.
NotificationsRequested only if you use payment reminders or recurring-invoice alerts, to show a local notification on your device.
Boot completedLets previously scheduled reminders re-arm after your device restarts.

InvoicePro does not request contacts, SMS, call log, location, or broad storage/file access. Photos (logo, signature, receipts) are picked through Android's own system photo picker; backup and export files are saved through Android's own document picker — in both cases the app never gets open-ended access to your files or photo library.

Advertising

The free version of InvoicePro shows ads served by Google AdMob. To load and personalize ads (subject to your consent choice below), Google and its advertising partners may collect an advertising identifier, general device information, and coarse usage signals, and may set identifiers for measurement and fraud prevention. This happens under Google's own privacy terms, not ours:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data when you use AdMob apps

On first launch, users in the EEA, UK, and other regions where it applies are shown a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) to choose whether personalized ads are allowed. You can change this choice from Settings → Privacy at any time.

Ads never appear during onboarding, invoice editing, payment entry, or PDF preview/sharing, and never for users who have unlocked Premium.

InvoicePro has no in-app purchases. "Premium" (no ads, all templates, PDF reports) is unlocked for 24 hours by choosing to watch three rewarded ads — this is optional and always initiated by you tapping a clearly labelled button.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not operate any backend that receives your invoices, clients, or business information. The only third party involved is Google AdMob/UMP, as described above.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it yourself — by removing individual invoices/clients/expenses in the app, clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstalling the app. Because we never hold a copy, we cannot delete data on your behalf; uninstalling the app removes it entirely.

Children's privacy

InvoicePro is a business tool intended for adults running or managing a business, and is not directed at children.
